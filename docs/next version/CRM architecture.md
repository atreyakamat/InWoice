# INWOICE - CRM, CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE & RELATIONSHIP ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT

## Introduction

This document defines the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) architecture of InWoice.

The CRM layer exists to ensure that every customer interaction, purchase, payment, communication, campaign response, and business relationship is captured, organized, analyzed, and transformed into actionable intelligence.

Most businesses only know:

Who their customers are.

InWoice should know:

Who their customers are.

What they buy.

Why they buy.

When they buy.

How often they buy.

How valuable they are.

How likely they are to return.

How likely they are to leave.

What actions should be taken next.

The CRM architecture transforms customer records into customer intelligence.

---

# CRM Philosophy

The purpose of CRM is not customer storage.

The purpose of CRM is customer understanding.

A customer should never be viewed as:

Name

Phone Number

Email

Instead, a customer should be viewed as:

Relationship

Revenue Source

Growth Opportunity

Marketing Opportunity

Retention Opportunity

Business Intelligence Source

Every customer profile should continuously become more valuable as the relationship grows.

# INWOICE - ACCOUNTING, FINANCE & BUSINESS HEALTH ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT

## Introduction

This document defines the Accounting, Finance, Revenue Management, Expense Tracking, Profitability Analysis, Taxation, Cash Flow, and Financial Intelligence architecture of InWoice.

Accounting is often treated as a separate system from sales and customer management.

InWoice takes a different approach.

Accounting is not isolated.

Accounting is the financial reflection of every activity happening across the business.

Every invoice.

Every payment.

Every expense.

Every campaign.

Every product.

Every customer.

Ultimately impacts financial health.

The purpose of this architecture is to ensure that financial intelligence is generated automatically as business activity occurs.

Business owners should spend less time preparing reports and more time making decisions.

---

# Financial Philosophy

Most businesses maintain financial records only for compliance.

InWoice should use financial information as a decision-making tool.

The goal is not simply to know:

How much money came in.

The goal is to know:

Why money came in.

Where money came from.

Which activities generated profit.

Which activities generated losses.

What actions should be taken next.

The financial system should continuously explain the economic reality of the business.

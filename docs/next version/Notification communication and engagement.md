# INWOICE - NOTIFICATION, COMMUNICATION & ENGAGEMENT ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT

## Introduction

This document defines how InWoice handles communication, notifications, customer engagement, reminders, campaigns, messaging history, customer interactions, and future omnichannel communication.

Communication is one of the most overlooked business systems.

Businesses often use:

WhatsApp

Email

Instagram

Phone Calls

SMS

Notes

Spreadsheets

Without a centralized record.

As a result:

Conversations get lost.

Follow-ups are forgotten.

Customers become inactive.

Opportunities disappear.

The purpose of this architecture is to ensure that every business conversation becomes part of the operational memory of the company.

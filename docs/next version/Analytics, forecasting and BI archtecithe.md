# INWOICE - ANALYTICS, FORECASTING & BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT

## Introduction

This document defines the Analytics and Business Intelligence layer of InWoice.

Data alone does not create value.

Reports alone do not create value.

Dashboards alone do not create value.

Understanding creates value.

The purpose of the Analytics Layer is to transform operational data into business intelligence.

Every invoice.

Every payment.

Every customer interaction.

Every product purchase.

Every campaign.

Every business event.

Should contribute toward a growing understanding of the business.

This document defines how InWoice becomes an intelligence platform rather than a record-keeping platform.

---

# Analytics Philosophy

The purpose of analytics is not to show numbers.

The purpose of analytics is to improve decisions.

The platform should answer:

What happened?

Why did it happen?

What will happen next?

What should be done about it?

# INWOICE - PRODUCT CATALOG, PACK MANAGEMENT & COMMERCE ARCHITECTURE DOCUMENT

## Introduction

This document defines the complete architecture for Products, Packs, Catalog Management, Commercial Offerings, Pricing Systems, and future Commerce capabilities within InWoice.

For most businesses, products are the core assets that generate revenue.

For Stix N Vibes specifically, products and packs represent the foundation upon which invoices, customer relationships, analytics, marketing, and future inventory systems are built.

This architecture exists to ensure that every product, sticker design, collection, bundle, service, and future offering can be managed through a centralized system.

The Product and Pack layer is not simply a catalog.

It is the commercial engine of the business.

---

Most small businesses think of products as things they sell.

Over time, products become increasingly intelligent business objects.

# Pack Versioning System

## Purpose

Prevent historical issues.

If a pack changes later, historical invoices must remain accurate.

---

## Example

Anime Starter Pack V1

Contains:

10 Naruto Stickers

10 One Piece Stickers

---

Anime Starter Pack V2

Contains:

15 Naruto Stickers

10 One Piece Stickers

5 Demon Slayer Stickers

---

Old invoices must preserve V1.

New invoices use V2.

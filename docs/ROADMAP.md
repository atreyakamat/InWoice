# Status & Roadmap

## v4.0: a business engine (Oct 2026)

v4.0 groups the v3.5 to v3.9 work. InWoice now covers the whole money cycle, can be hosted
for many businesses from one server, and suggests the day's jobs.

**v3.5, selling and getting paid**
- **Quotations/estimates** with their own numbering, PDF and email. One click converts a quote
  to an invoice.
- **Recurring invoices** (weekly/monthly/quarterly/yearly) are generated by the scheduler.
  They can be issued and emailed automatically, and missed runs catch up safely (at most 12).
- **Customer portal:** a private link where the customer sees their invoices, balance and
  statement and can download PDFs. Links can be revoked.
- **Statements of account** as PDF and email, plus optional monthly statements to every
  customer with a balance.
- **Forgot/reset password** by email, and `npm run reset-password` for the server owner.

**v3.6, staff and safety**
- **Roles:** Admin, Staff, Sales, Stores and Accountant, each with its own menu and API
  access. Changing a role or password signs that user out everywhere.
- **Two-factor sign-in** (authenticator app, TOTP) with one-time recovery codes.
- **Activity log:** who created, changed, cancelled or deleted what, with filters. Kept for
  `AUDIT_RETENTION_DAYS` (default 3 years).
- **Period lock:** once GST is filed, nothing dated on or before the lock date can be added,
  changed or cancelled.
- **Off-site backups** to any S3-compatible storage (AWS, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2,
  Wasabi, MinIO) after each daily backup, with a test button.

**v3.7, hosting many businesses (SaaS)**
- One server, many businesses. Each business gets its own PostgreSQL schema and is reached at
  `<slug>.BASE_DOMAIN` or its own domain. Every business's schema is migrated at startup.
- Optional public sign-up with a free trial, and plans (Starter/Growth/Pro) with user and
  monthly invoice limits. An expired or suspended account becomes read-only.
- A platform admin page (`/platform`) and `npm run platform` (create admin, create business,
  adopt an existing single-business database, list, migrate).

**v3.8, accounts depth**
- **TDS:** TDS deducted by customers on receipts and by you on supplier payments (with
  section), plus a TDS summary for 26AS/Form 16A checks.
- **Supplier debit notes** (purchase returns and rate differences), with GST reversal, stock
  out and refunds due.
- **Bank statement import** (CSV from any Indian bank, columns guessed). Lines auto-match to
  payments, bill payments and expenses, and the rest can be matched by hand or turned into
  expenses.
- **GSTR-1 JSON** for the GST portal's offline tool, and a **GSTR-2B check** that matches
  the portal's 2B JSON against your bills.
- **Stock valuation:** daily stock snapshots and opening/closing stock in the P&L.

**v3.9, AI that does the work (with approval)**
- **Read a bill from a photo or PDF:** the supplier, bill number, dates, lines and GST are
  filled in for you to check before saving. Totals are cross-checked and differences are
  flagged.
- **Actions page:** chase overdue invoices (AI-written, editable emails), reorder low
  materials (draft purchase orders per supplier, quantities editable), and re-send quotes
  about to expire. Nothing runs until you approve the exact list. Works without AI, using
  templates.
- **Monthly owner summary** emailed on the 1st: sales, profit, cash, GST to pay and who to
  chase, with a short AI note when AI is on.
- **Operator alerts:** server errors, crashes and failed scheduled jobs are posted to
  `ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL` (Slack/Discord), throttled. `/health` reports version, uptime, memory
  and the last error.

**Tests:** 276 backend tests (72 of them pure unit tests that run in seconds) and 41
browser checks.

## v3.4: money out and accounts (Oct 2026)

InWoice now covers both sides of the business, so an owner can run day-to-day money in it
without a separate expenses spreadsheet.

- **Supplier bills:** from a received purchase order (lines pre-filled, no double stock) or
  standalone (can add materials to stock). GST split from the supplier's GSTIN/state,
  claimable vs blocked input credit, due dates from supplier terms, part payments, and what
  you owe each supplier. Edits and cancellations keep stock exact.
- **Expenses:** rent, salaries, utilities and so on, entered with or without GST, with
  categories, vendor GSTIN and input credit.
- **Accounts page:** profit & loss by category and month (CSV), GSTR-3B summary with the
  legal set-off order and cash payable, receivables and payables ageing, cash in/out and
  what falls due in the next 7/30 days. Purchase register CSV for the accountant.
- **Dashboard and insights:** profit this month, spending, amounts owed and overdue bills.
  The health score's profit figure now uses the real 90-day margin once costs are recorded,
  and overdue supplier bills and losses show up as risks and in the daily briefing.
- **Tests:** 41 new unit tests (pure rules, no database, < 1 s, `npm run test:unit`) and 30
  new API tests built around one hand-checked scenario. 161 tests in total, plus 29 browser
  checks. The phone-layout check now also catches tables pushing cards off-screen; it found
  and fixed that on the dashboard and other pages.
- Demo data (`npm run seed`) includes bills and expenses, so the new screens aren't empty
  in a sales demo.

## v3.3: compliance and getting paid (Oct 2026)

Four of the five "needed to sell" items below are done:

- **GST reports (GSTR-1):** B2B, B2C large, B2C small, credit notes (CDNR/CDNUR), HSN
  summary split B2B/B2C, and documents issued, for any month or date range. CSVs follow
  the GST offline tool templates. There's also a line-level sales register. Built-in GST
  state codes mean "TN", "Tamil Nadu" and a `33…` GSTIN all count as the same state. When
  a customer has no state, it comes from their GSTIN. GSTINs are checksum-validated, so
  typos are caught at entry.
- **Credit notes:** returns (with optional restock), post-sale discounts and corrections,
  with GST reversed at the original rates. Refunds due are calculated automatically.
  Credit notes have their own numbering and PDF. Balances, revenue, customer value and the
  dashboard are all net of credit notes.
- **Automatic payment reminders:** emails go out on a schedule relative to the due date,
  with the PDF and UPI QR attached and no daily spam. Manual "Send reminder" button.
  WhatsApp messages for overdue invoices use reminder wording.
- **Backups:** an automatic daily backup on the server (newest 14 kept), backup now,
  download a copy, and restore from Settings or the command line. The restore is
  checked by tests (export → change → restore gives identical data) and by restoring
  the dev database into a scratch database.
- Tests: 90 API tests (was 52) and 22 browser checks. The test suite runs in about 10 s
  instead of 3 minutes on a busy machine (`isolate: false`).

## v3.2: what changed (Oct 2026)

The TypeScript/Prisma rewrite (v4/v5 work in progress) did not compile or start. The
frontend had a broken `App.tsx`, imports of UI components that didn't exist, hard-coded
`localhost:5000` URLs, and no invoice screens. v3.2 rebuilds both on **PostgreSQL** and
brings the core v3 business features (invoices, GST, PDF, payments) into the new stack.

**Backend**

- Rewrote the API with consistent validation (zod), central error handling and transactions.
- Added the missing sales core: invoices end to end, GST engine, PDF with UPI QR, email,
  WhatsApp links, payments, CSV exports.
- Fixed ERP logic:
  - Stock deduction is idempotent and reversible.
  - Production checks materials before consuming them.
  - Purchase-order receiving updates status correctly.
  - Event returns go back into stock.
- Replaced placeholder "virtual C-suite" calls with real SQL analytics and a rule engine that
  works without AI. AI is now optional: Claude or Ollama.
- Security:
  - First-run setup replaces open registration.
  - Roles added, and every route is authenticated (suppliers and production were open before).
  - Rate limiting, Helmet/CSP, and no secrets sent to the browser.
- Lighter footprint:
  - Removed Puppeteer (headless Chrome) and native bcrypt.
  - PDFs are pure JS.
  - One process serves everything (~86 MB RAM).
- Added a seed script, one consolidated migration, and 52 integration tests.

**Frontend**

- Rebuilt every screen: dashboard, invoices (list/editor/detail), customers, products, BOM,
  packs, inventory, suppliers, purchase orders, production, fulfillment, events, insights/AI,
  and settings (7 tabs).
- Shared design system, mobile-responsive layout, lazy-loaded pages (~110 KB initial gzip).
- Real-browser smoke test (`npm --prefix frontend run e2e`).

**Bugs caught by the new tests during the rebuild**

- Saving one Settings tab would have wiped the other tabs' fields (absent form fields
  were treated as "clear").
- A completed setup returned 400 instead of 403 when called again.
- The invoice PDF overlapped the customer address with the items table.
- An independent code review of the new screens found more, all fixed:
  - The Settings invoicing tab could roll the invoice counter back, which would have
    blocked new invoices. The server now also skips numbers already in use.
  - Editing a product could overwrite stock with a stale value.
  - A failed "Produce now" left an orphan batch behind.
  - Duplicate materials on one purchase order could never be fully received.
  - Shipment notes could be lost.

## Known gaps / limitations

- Money is stored as `Float` with 2-decimal rounding. `Decimal` would be stricter for
  accounting, but it means changing every money column and calculation. Planned as its own
  release, with a data migration and the full test suite as the safety net.
- Prisma is on 5.22. Moving to the next major version changes the client generator and
  config, so it is a separate upgrade with its own testing pass.
- GSTR-1 JSON and the GSTR-2B check follow the published offline-tool formats. Validate the
  first upload of each new client in the offline tool.
- Refunds owed to customers (on credit notes) are not yet recorded as money out in the cash
  summary.
- The P&L is a management view. Your accountant still prepares the statutory books (no
  double-entry ledger yet).
- No debit notes to customers (increasing an issued invoice). Issue a second invoice instead.
- Report periods, reminder days and backup names use the server's time zone. Set `TZ`
  (default `Asia/Kolkata`).
- Customer pickers load up to 500 customers. That needs server-side search past that point.
- Email and AI calls are synchronous (no job queue). That's fine at small-business volume.
- Reading bills from images works with Claude (photos and PDFs) and with local vision
  models in Ollama (photos only).
- No automated frontend unit tests. Coverage comes from type-checking, the shared-rules
  parity tests and the browser smoke test.

   "Paid" automatically by webhook. Needs your Razorpay account details.

================================================================================
INWOICE v2.1 - COMPREHENSIVE IMPLEMENTED FEATURES LIST
================================================================================

1. CORE INVOICE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Full CRUD Operations: Create, Read, Update, and Delete invoices.
- Invoice Cloning: One-click duplication of existing invoices.
- Advanced Search & Filtering: Filter by date, status (Paid/Pending), and customer.
- Multi-Status Tracking: Monitor payment life cycles (Draft, Pending, Paid, Overdue).
- UPI QR Code Generation: Automatic generation of payment QR codes on invoices.
- PDF Generation Engine: High-performance, branded PDF creation (Puppeteer-based).
- PDF Caching: 80% faster generation for frequently accessed invoices.
- Product Catalog: Reusable product templates with auto-pricing and description.
- Smart Tax Calculation: Automated GST and discount calculations.
- Bulk Actions: Export and manage multiple invoices simultaneously.

2. CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP MANAGEMENT (CRM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Automatic Record Creation: Customer profiles created automatically upon invoicing.
- Detailed Customer Profiles: Full purchase history, lifetime value, and engagement metrics.
- AI-Driven Segmentation: Auto-categorization of customers:
  * VIP (High revenue)
  * Loyal (High order frequency)
  * Regular (Consistent activity)
  * New (First-time buyers)
  * At Risk (Inactive for >90 days)
- Customer Lifecycle Tracking: Days since last purchase and average days to pay.
- CRM Search: Find customers by name, email, or Instagram handle.
- Retention Analytics: Track customer churn and loyalty trends.

3. AI AUTOMATION & INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Voice-to-Invoice Parsing: Transform voice notes into structured invoice data.
- OCR Scanner: Extract data from physical documents and receipts.
- WhatsApp Order Extraction: Automatically pull order details from chat messages.
- Hybrid AI Backend: Support for Local AI (Ollama/Python) and Cloud AI (Google Gemini).
- Intelligent Search: Semantic search capabilities for products and customers.
- Automated Categorization: AI-assisted tagging of expenses and income.

4. ACCOUNTING & FINANCIAL TOOLS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Double-Entry Ledger: Professional accounting system for all transactions.
- Bank Reconciliation: Match bank statements with internal records (via OCR/Import).
- GST Reporting: Automated generation of tax reports for compliance.
- Expense Tracking: Record and categorize business expenses.
- Profit & Loss (P&L): Real-time financial health dashboards.
- Payment Method Distribution: Track revenue across UPI, Bank Transfer, Cash, and Card.

5. ANALYTICS & REPORTING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Interactive Dashboard: Real-time revenue trends and growth metrics.
- Sales Forecasting: Trend-based projections for future revenue.
- Product Performance: Analysis of best-selling and most profitable items.
- Cohort Analysis: Behavior tracking of customer groups over time.
- Conversion Tracking: Lead-to-sale metrics for marketing campaigns.
- Export Center: CSV downloads for Invoices, Customers, and Products.

6. INTEGRATIONS & EXTERNAL SYNC
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Google Sheets Cloud Sync: Real-time backup and secondary storage.
- WhatsApp Sharing: Direct sharing of invoice links and PDFs via WhatsApp.
- Email Client: Integrated SMTP client for sending invoices and marketing emails.
- IMAP Integration: Sync emails for order tracking and communication history.
- Offline-First Architecture: Local SQLite/JSON storage with later sync capability.
- Web Invoice View: Secure, public links for customers to view invoices online.

7. MARKETING & TASK MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Marketing Campaigns: Email/WhatsApp campaign management.
- Lead Tracking: Manage potential customers before they make a purchase.
- Integrated Task Manager: Kanban-style task tracking for business operations.
- Customer Feedback: Tooling for gathering and storing customer reviews.

8. SECURITY & ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- JWT Authentication: Secure, token-based login with auto-expiry.
- Enterprise-Grade Encryption: Bcrypt password hashing (10 rounds).
- Rate Limiting: Protection against brute-force and DDoS attacks.
- Secure API Headers: Helmet.js and CORS protection.
- Input Validation: Strict Zod schema validation for all API requests.
- Advanced Logging: Winston-based file rotation with separate error/combined logs.
- Environment Isolation: Strict separation of development and production configs.
